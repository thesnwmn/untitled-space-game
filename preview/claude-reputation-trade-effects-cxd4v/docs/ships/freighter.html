Standard Freighter

The workhorse of civilian space. Underpowered, overloaded, and still flying after twenty years — the Standard Freighter is the ship most independent pilots start with and many never replace. Slow to accelerate and vulnerable in a fight, but the cargo hold earns its keep on the Core Routes.
