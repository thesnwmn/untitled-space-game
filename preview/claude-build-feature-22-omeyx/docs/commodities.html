Commodities

The interstellar economy runs on the movement of goods between systems that produce them and stations that need them.

Prices listed are base values — actual trading prices shift with system supply, demand, and the goods_bias of the local station. Contraband commands high prices in core systems but drawing attention to yourself is a risk all its own.
