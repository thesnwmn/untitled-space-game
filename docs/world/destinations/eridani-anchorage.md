# Eridani Anchorage

Out in the belt, away from Keelhaul's clean lines and military protocols, Eridani
Anchorage is the part of Epsilon Eridani that independent captains can actually
use. The station is older and louder than anything in the inner system — worn
plating, patched airlocks, the satisfying chaos of a working port that grew by
accretion rather than design. The Eridani Colonial Council runs it, which means
the rules are practical rather than procedural.

Warden Fleck manages the trade floor with the blunt efficiency of someone who
processes ore manifests in volume every day. Raw materials move fast; refined
goods move at a slight premium. The mining contracts that supply Keelhaul's
construction yards mostly pass through Fleck's books at some point.

The mission board is the Council's domain: belt extraction contracts, equipment
delivery to outlying claim sites, and the occasional salvage job for debris from
the active stellar environment. Flare advisories are posted at the board
alongside the contracts — not optional reading.

Repairs here are honest. The engineering team works on whatever arrives and quotes
accurately. They're not as fast as Keelhaul's yards, but they're not running on
a permit clock either. Pilots who treat the Anchorage well tend to find subsequent
visits a little smoother. Belt communities remember.
