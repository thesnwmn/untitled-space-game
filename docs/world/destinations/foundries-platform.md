# Foundries Platform

Docking at the Foundries Platform means committing to the noise. The platform is
an interlocked chain of manufacturing structures threaded through the inner
asteroid belt — airlocks that connect to pressurised work floors, corridors that
smell of hot metal and ozone, and constant vibration from the fabrication equipment
that never stops running. It is not a comfortable place, but it is an efficient one.

Steward Karras runs the works supply exchange: raw materials in, components out,
with the brisk transactional manner of someone whose time is measured in kilotons.
The mission board here deals almost exclusively in industrial contracts — ore
delivery, component hauling, supply runs to specific platform sections. The repair
bays are staffed by engineers who treat spacecraft like large machines requiring
maintenance, which is technically accurate and emotionally deflating.

Access requires a works permit, issued by either the Union or the League. The
processing office is on Meridian Station. Captains who show up without one are
turned around politely but without exception.
