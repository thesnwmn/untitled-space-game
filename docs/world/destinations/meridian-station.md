# Meridian Station

A large torus orbital in Sirius's outer system, far enough from the star's
radiation bands to be comfortable and close enough to the Foundries to keep
freight moving efficiently. Meridian handles everything: passengers, bulk cargo,
industrial components, and the continuous paperwork that the Centauri Trade
League generates by existing.

Syndic Havel's trading floor occupies the station's commercial ring — a wide,
well-lit space that hums with the sound of commodity negotiations conducted at
polite volume. Prices track the Alpha Centauri exchange with minimal lag; the
League keeps the data feeds current. Independent pilots who check the boards
carefully will find Meridian one of the better-connected markets in the outer
core, but the League's commission rates are visible in every transaction.

Docking is efficient and slightly impersonal. The station staff are professional,
the queues are managed, and the sense is of a machine that works because everyone
involved understands their role in it.
