# Ceti Landfall

The main surface port sits on a plateau in Ceti Prime's northern continent,
surrounded by the managed landscape of large-scale agriculture. The horizon in
every direction is cropland or processing infrastructure — the visual scale of
the operation is something that takes time to absorb. Ships descend through thin
atmosphere and land on a wide tarmac that smells of mineral dust and something
vegetative that no one from the inner systems can quite name.

Broker Senne handles the trade floor with the easy confidence of someone who
knows exactly what their system produces and how much the inner systems need it.
Agricultural exports make up the majority of the manifest — the pricing reflects
both genuine quality and the mild leverage of being the closest farming world
to Sol. Incoming supply goods move at fair rates. Senne doesn't argue over
reasonable offers; the volumes here make margins less important than throughput.

The ship dealer at Landfall keeps an inventory of heavy haulers and surface-capable
freighters suited to the work the system actually requires. Military specifications
are absent; reliability and cargo volume are not. For pilots looking to buy a
practical working ship rather than a fast one, Ceti Landfall is one of the better
options in the outer core.

Ship repairs are not available on the surface. Captains needing structural work
are directed to Waypoint Ceti.
