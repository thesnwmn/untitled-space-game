# Alpha Centauri System

The Alpha Centauri system is humanity’s first and largest interstellar colony, consisting of the twin stars Alpha Centauri A & B and the distant red dwarf Proxima Centauri. Featuring vibrant settlements, a complex political landscape, and cutting-edge technology, this system acts as both a gateway and a battleground for major factions expanding from Sol.

## Notable bodies

### Proxima Centauri b
A tidally locked world orbiting Proxima Centauri, home to New Alexandria—a domed capital city and scientific hub for exoplanetary research.

### Alpha Centauri Bb & Ac
Terraforming work is in progress on terrestrial planets in the primary binary, largely managed by Helios Conglomerate, with initial settlements in semi-habitable domes.

### Prometheus Belt
The asteroid belt between Alpha Centauri A and B, heavily mined for rare fusion elements and patrolled by competing security forces.

## Notable destinations

### Proxima Station
A massive, rotating torus—primary trade, immigration, and defense node, jointly operated by the major corporate interests.

### New Alexandria
A domed city on Proxima b, known for its cosmopolitan administration, research universities, and open intrigue between Helios Conglomerate and Neo-Commonwealth agents.

### Prometheus Belt Outpost
A network of habitats used by miners and smugglers. Controlled by the Wayfarers Cooperative, it's a stopover for ships heading to Barnard’s Star.

## Governance

Alpha Centauri’s governance is in constant flux. The Helios Conglomerate claims much of the system through corporate charter, while the Neo-Commonwealth agitates for democratic reforms. The independent Wayfarers Cooperative mediates trade and smuggling, leveraging outposts in the Prometheus Belt and links to Barnard’s Star.

## History

Discovered and colonized in the 2080s, Alpha Centauri became a technological showcase for humanity, enduring early ecological catastrophes and sabotage from Sol-based rivals. By 2214, its rapid growth has made it the keystone of interstellar economy and politics, but internal tensions simmer between capitalist, collective, and populist visions.

## Local reputation

Travelers see Alpha Centauri as a melting pot rife with opportunity and danger. Locals are fiercely proud but wary of outsiders—especially agents of rival factions. Expect tight security around corporate assets and freedom (or chaos) in the outer belts.
